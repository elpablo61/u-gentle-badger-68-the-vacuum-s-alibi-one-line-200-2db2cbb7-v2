One line, at most 200 characters: the excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot.

> My cliff sensor flagged the dust bunny as a ledge, so I filed a safety report and went home early.

`main.ts` (node22) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. The line was picked by pairwise judging of 5 fresh variants against the house judge's brief: deadpan machine voice, a real joke that lands, surprise in a specific detail, punch at the end.
